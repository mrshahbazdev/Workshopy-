Workshopy — Privacy Policy

Effective: 2026 · Publisher: Muhammad Shahbaz (mrshahbaznns@gmail.com)

What Workshopy stores

Workshopy keeps workshop records you enter: customer names, phone numbers and addresses, vehicle plates and service history, parts stock, job cards, prices and payment receipts.

Where it is stored

All records are stored only on the computer running Workshopy, in a locally encrypted (SQLCipher) database whose key is protected by the operating system's credential store. The app makes no internet requests — nothing is uploaded to us or to any third party, and there are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking.

Who controls it

The workshop is the data controller. The publisher of Workshopy has no access to your records. The workshop is responsible for any required consent, for access control (user PINs and roles are provided in the app), and for backups.

Optional LAN sharing

An optional, user-initiated LAN mode can share the app with a second office PC on the same local network. It is off by default, protected by an access code, and never connects to the internet.

Backups

Manual and scheduled backups are written only to folders you choose (e.g. a USB drive). Their handling is the workshop's responsibility.
